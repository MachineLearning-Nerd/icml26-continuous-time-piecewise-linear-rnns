# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Alena Brändle, Lukas Eisenmann, Florian Götz, and Daniel Durstewitz for developing a continuous-time extension of PLRNNs, exposing the regional analytic and switching-time procedures, and documenting the equilibrium, limit-cycle, and benchmark protocols.

This repository is an independent, scoped audit. It preserves the paper’s source identity, cites the original work, records how each claim is produced, and clearly separates the two bounded structural toys from benchmark results that remain unverified locally.
